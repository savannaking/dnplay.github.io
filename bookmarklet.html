AncestryDNA Bookmarklet

A one-click version of the AncestryDNA Result Displayer. No codes to copy and nothing to paste.

Click it on any ancestry.com page while you're signed in. It loads your chromosome painter percentages (with decimals), your ethnicity estimate and its ranges, and shows them in a panel on top of the page. Your data only goes between your browser and Ancestry.

Step 1: Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

DNPlay

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).

Step 2: Go to ancestry.com, sign in, and click the DNPlay bookmark.

Click it again or press Esc to close the panel. To see someone else's results that you manage, open one of their DNA pages first.
